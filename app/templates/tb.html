Prior-year comparatives

The comparative column of the Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss and Notes comes from each ledger's prior-year closing balance. Enter it the way it is normally shown: assets and expenses as positive debits, liabilities, income and equity as positive credits (a minus sign is the opposite side). The previous year's profit is added to its reserves automatically. Saving updates the statements, notes, checks and materiality at once.

Two columns: Ledger Name, Prior Year Amount. The name must match the trial balance.
