Fixed deposit register

List the bank fixed deposits, then post them: each FD becomes one approved journal entry that moves its principal out of the bank ledger onto the agreed Schedule III line (current or non-current) and books the interest accrued in the year as Other income and Interest accrued. Accrued interest is simple interest for the days inside the financial year (principal × rate × days ÷ 365); a figure you enter is checked against it. Post only FDs and interest that are not already in the trial balance. A Checker or Partner posts, never the person who entered the rows.
