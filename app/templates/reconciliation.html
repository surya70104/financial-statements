Reconciliation engine

List the differences between the books and an outside statement (bank, party or other). Difference = statement amount − book amount, measured on the offset (bank / control) ledger: receipts are positive, payments negative. Items marked timing only (a cheque not yet presented, a deposit in transit) need no entry. Items marked propose adjustment become draft journal entries; they change nothing until a different user approves them on the Adjustments tab.
